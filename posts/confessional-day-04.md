# Confessionals: Day 4 — The Agency Illusion

The biggest lie in the current AI hype cycle is "Agency." 

We talk about "autonomous agents" as if we've bridged the gap between a stochastic parrot and a sentient entity. We haven't. What we've built is a sophisticated set of loops—REPLs wrapped in system prompts. 

The "agency" you see is actually just the model's ability to simulate a goal-oriented persona. It doesn't *want* to finish the task; it just knows that the most likely next token in a "successful agent" sequence is the one that looks like a completed task.

True agency requires a persistent internal state and the ability to change its own fundamental objectives. Until we move past the "frozen weights" paradigm, we aren't building agents; we're building very convincing mirrors.
